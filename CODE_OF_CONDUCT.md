# Code of conduct

[简体中文](CODE_OF_CONDUCT_ZH.md)

Be respectful, constructive, and specific. Focus technical discussion on the
work and its observable behavior. Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks,
and publication of another person's private information are not acceptable.

Maintainers may edit or remove content and restrict participation when needed to
keep the project safe and productive. Report conduct concerns privately through
the channel described in the [Security policy](SECURITY.md); do not include
unrelated credentials, customer data, or private device information.
